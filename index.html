Your Gateway to Sri Lanka's Wildlife

From the world-famous elephant gatherings to elusive leopards and vibrant birdlife, every safari is crafted to bring you closer to nature while supporting conservation efforts.

Explore Nature

Our Three Signature Parks

Matale District

Minneriya National Park

Famous for "The Gathering" – the largest congregation of Asian elephants in the world. Over 300 elephants gather around the ancient Minneriya Tank during the dry season.

Best: June - Sept From $45
Polonnaruwa District

Kaudulla National Park

A serene sanctuary connecting elephant corridors. Kaudulla offers peaceful safari experiences with excellent birdwatching opportunities and fewer crowds than Minneriya.

Best: Aug - Dec From $40
Habarana

Hurulu Eco Park

A designated biosphere reserve with dense dry evergreen forest. Perfect for spotting elephants, leopards, and endemic bird species in a pristine natural setting.

Best: Year Round From $35
